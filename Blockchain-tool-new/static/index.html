CASE TOOLKIT

On-Chain Investigations

A blockchain forensics toolkit for tracing stolen crypto and building court-ready evidence - built for investigators working with law enforcement.

⇢ Link Tracer

Traces funds hop by hop from a wallet, looks up a known transaction hash directly, or searches a wallet's activity around a specific date.

⛃ Deposit Mapping

Confirms exchange deposit addresses by watching for consolidation sweeps.

⇄ Swap/Bridge Check

Finds where funds likely went after passing through a non-KYC exchange or bridge - even across chains.

Every finding is labeled by confidence - confirmed, heuristic, or lead - nothing is ever presented as proof it isn't.
