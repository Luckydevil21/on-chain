On-Chain Investigations

A blockchain forensics toolkit for tracing stolen crypto and building court-ready evidence - built for investigators working with law enforcement. Covers Ethereum, Bitcoin, XRP, Tron, Solana, and major EVM chains (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, and more).

⇢ Link Tracer

Traces funds hop by hop across six chains, looks up a known transaction hash directly (even years-old ones), or searches a wallet's activity around a specific date.

⛃ Deposit Mapping

Confirms exchange deposit addresses by watching for consolidation sweeps.

⇄ Swap/Bridge Check

Finds where funds likely went after passing through a non-KYC exchange or bridge - even across chains.

⚠ Token/Contract Risk Check

Checks a Solana token or EVM contract for real, verifiable red flags - active mint/freeze authority, unrenounced ownership, upgradeable proxies.

▤ Saved Cases

Group wallets and evidence under named cases you can revisit, rather than one flat, ungrouped list.

📄 Evidence Pack

Generates a court-ready PDF with full methodology, a SHA-256 integrity hash, and optional Bitcoin blockchain timestamp anchoring.

Every finding is labeled by confidence - confirmed, heuristic, or lead - nothing is ever presented as proof it isn't.
