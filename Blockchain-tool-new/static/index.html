CASE TOOLKIT

On-Chain Investigations

A blockchain forensics toolkit for tracing stolen crypto, identifying victims, and building court-ready evidence - built for investigators working with law enforcement.

◎ Wallet Watcher

Monitors flagged wallets across ETH/BTC/XRP and alerts the moment they move funds.

◈ Victim Collator

Given a scammer's wallet, finds everyone who sent money into it.

⇢ Link Tracer

Traces funds forward or backward, hop by hop, across multiple wallets automatically.

⛃ Deposit Mapping

Confirms exchange deposit addresses by watching for consolidation sweeps.

Every finding is labeled by confidence - confirmed, heuristic, or lead - nothing is ever presented as proof it isn't.
